## Content Strategy

### Posting Schedule
- **3 videos per week**: Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday
- **Time**: Always 7:30 AM
- **Sustainability**: This pace works with a full-time job (no burnout)

### Content Mix (60/40 Rule)

**Educational/Value Content (60-70%)**:
- Problem-solving tips ("Why does your dough stick?")
- Technique explanations ("How to tell if dough is ready")
- Recipe breakdowns
- Common mistakes and fixes
- **Result**: Followers + Saves + Long-term engagement

**Food Porn Content (30-40%)**:
- ASMR crunchy crust sounds
- Cheese pulls
- Pizza coming out of oven
- Visual satisfaction moments
- **Result**: Views + Likes (but fewer followers)

**Strategy**: Prioritize educational content for follower growth, use food porn for reach

---

## Video Specifications

### Length & Format
- **8-12 seconds maximum**
- **Completion rate > Engagement** (people must watch to the end)
- **18+ seconds** = high drop-off rate
- Vertical format (9:16) for TikTok/Reels

### The Hook (First 2 Seconds)
Must grab attention immediately:
- **Direct problem**: "למה הבצק נדבק?" (Why does your dough stick?)
- **Challenge**: "תנסו לא להתרעב" (Try not to get hungry)
- **ASMR**: Sound of crunchy crust
- **Curiosity**: "הסוד לבצק מושלם" (The secret to perfect dough)

### Content Types to Create
✅ Educational tips
✅ Problem-solving
✅ Technique demonstrations
✅ Food porn (ASMR, visuals)
✅ Behind-the-scenes (occasionally)

❌ No hard selling of dough/kits (doesn't work on TikTok)
❌ No long-form content (not sustainable)

## Core Principles

1. **Authenticity over perfection** - Real beats polished
2. **Value first, sales later** - Build trust before promoting
3. **Consistency over intensity** - 3/week sustainable beats daily burnout
4. **Educational priority** - Followers > Views
5. **Hebrew, always** - Speak your audience's language
6. **Passionate, not pretentious** - You love this stuff, let it show
